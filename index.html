Digital Tasbeeh Privacy Policy

Developer: MrJ Group LLC
Last updated: September 21, 2026

Digital Tasbeeh is a dhikr counter and prayer-reminder app. This policy explains how the app uses information and how to request deletion. You can use the core counter without an account.

Information stored on your device

The app stores counter progress, counting sessions, custom dhikr and packages, favorites, guided-session progress, achievements, daily statistics, themes, sound and vibration preferences, and reminder settings locally. If you enable location features, it also saves your last selected coordinates and available city/country information. If you sign in, authentication session tokens are stored locally to keep you signed in.

Core counting, bundled dhikr, fonts, and locally saved progress work without internet access. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling the app may permanently remove local data.

Optional accounts and cloud synchronization

If you create an account, your email address and password are sent over HTTPS to Supabase for authentication. Supabase creates an account identifier. When signed in, the app synchronizes counting history, custom dhikr, targets, completion times, and daily statistics to the configured Supabase backend. These records may reveal information about your religious practices. Account creation and cloud synchronization are optional.

Network services receive connection information such as IP address, request times, and user-agent information. The operator must maintain appropriate access controls for the cloud database. No private server credential is distributed in the app.

Location and prayer reminders

Location access is requested when you choose Enable Location or Refresh on the prayer-reminder screen. Prayer times are calculated on your device. Coordinates are also sent over HTTPS to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to look up your city and country. Location accuracy depends on your device settings and permission choice. The app does not request background location access.

Notification permission is optional. Android schedules prayer reminders locally for a rolling period refreshed when the app opens. Reminder delivery can be delayed by device power-management settings. Vibration is used for optional haptic feedback.

Sharing

If you choose a Share action, the app passes the selected dhikr, achievement, or invitation text to the app you select in the system share sheet. That destination handles the information under its own policies. Sharing is not automatic.

Advertising, analytics, and purchases

This version contains no advertising SDK, advertising-ID access, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, subscriptions, or in-app purchase functionality. No account is needed for the core counter.

Service providers

  • Supabase: optional authentication and cloud database. Supabase privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation / Nominatim: city lookup using coordinates. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • GitHub: hosts this policy website and may receive ordinary website connection information. GitHub privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear it, uninstall the app, or use a deletion action. Cloud account records remain until deleted. You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud records by emailing designedbymrj@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Digital Tasbeeh account deletion.” Do not send your password. MrJ Group LLC may need to verify that you own the account before handling a request.

Deletion covers the account, custom cloud dhikr, counting sessions, daily statistics, and saved cloud reminders. Copies on other devices must be cleared on those devices. Service-provider security logs and backup copies may be retained according to the provider's settings, policies, and applicable requirements; contact us for information about a specific request. Support emails are handled separately from app records.

Your choices and security

You can use guest mode, revoke location or notification permission in Android settings, disable haptic feedback and sound, and request deletion. Production network requests use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security; please avoid entering confidential information into custom dhikr text.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The revision date appears above. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact MrJ Group LLC at designedbymrj@gmail.com. Website: designedbymrj.com.
