You can request deletion without reinstalling or opening the app.

  1. Email designedbymrj@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Digital Tasbeeh account.
  2. Use the subject Digital Tasbeeh account deletion and state that you want your account and associated cloud data deleted.
  3. Do not send your password. We may ask you to verify account ownership.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, compose a message to the address above manually.

What is deleted

Your account, custom cloud dhikr, counting sessions, daily statistics, and saved cloud reminders. Deletion is permanent. You can also request deletion of selected data by describing your request in the email.

Local data and retained copies

To remove counts, preferences, cached location, and other local records, open Android Settings → Apps → Digital Tasbeeh → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes local progress. Clear local data separately on each device.

Service-provider security logs and backup copies may remain according to the provider's settings, policies, and applicable requirements. Support correspondence is separate from app records. Contact the email above for information about processing or retention for your request.
